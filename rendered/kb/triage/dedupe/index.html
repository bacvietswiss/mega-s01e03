Triage: duplicate reports

owner support desk accepted reviewed 2026-10-28 · 0 days ago

A busy morning brings hundreds of reports about the same thing. We keep one ticket per issue and merge every duplicate into it, so engineering sees one thread with a count.

The rule

A report is a duplicate when the evidence supports the same cause as an open ticket (decision 0002, replacing decision 0001):

  1. preserve its ID, body, receipt time, and merge history;
  2. look up the tenant’s locale and booking form, then inspect the channel and symptoms;
  3. merge only into a ticket with a confirmed matching cause; otherwise keep it separate.
Subject lines help find candidates. They do not prove that two reports have the same cause. Keep the parser and booking-v3 hint problems in separate tickets.

When you are unsure

Keep the report separate and investigate. Unknown tenants and one-day anomalies need more evidence; do not silently absorb them into a known issue.
