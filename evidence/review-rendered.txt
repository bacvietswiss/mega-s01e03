Parser: day and month are read in the wrong order

The shared parser reads dates month first for tenants whose locale writes the day first. App and email reports, plus booking-v2 form reports for these tenants, belong here. The original ticket belongs to this majority cause: parser ticket.

Example: R-41203 reports 2 December → 12 February. The correction in R-41201 follows R-41200; they remain separate report IDs, not proof of two incidents. The fix should parse in the tenant’s intended date order and preserve the selected date through the invite. Verify ambiguous dates and dates whose day exceeds twelve.

Hint: booking-v3 tells day-first tenants to type month first

The booking-v3 form displays MM/DD/YYYY, while that form reads the input in the tenant’s day-first order. This affects form reports from alder.physio, copper.studio, and pale.harbour. It needs a separate ticket and a hint aligned with the form’s reader.

Example: R-41230, copper.studio, channel form, reports 6 January → 1 June. Intake tagged it parser; the tenant’s es-ES locale and booking-v3 form put it in the hint group. Track the fix in the form-hint ticket. Verify that the example, hint, accepted input, and saved date agree.

The one-day-move footnote is included in the parser total, not an extra group to add or subtract. Those reports do not establish a third root cause. Investigate them separately within the parser ticket rather than claiming that a date-order fix resolves them.

The desk’s notes name R-41202, R-41209, R-41236, R-41344, and R-41620. Their bodies differ: adjacent-day shifts, a June-only pattern, an invite arriving before booking creation, and Lisbon/Toronto displaying different days. Collect the intended date, event timestamp, original offset, tenant and viewer time zones, form version, and reproduction before attributing these symptoms to parsing or a time-zone defect.

How the counts are obtained

reports-4471.json is a paginated export with overlapping rows. uniqueById counts each ID once, then the page filters mergedInto === 4471. tenantOf joins the tenant directory; causeOf uses the tenant’s locale, form version, and report channel. groupByCause computes the table. Browser locale and meta.intakeTag are intake guesses, not the grouping key. An unknown tenant or unsupported locale stays unplaced; do not force a match.
